Those who drank caffeinated coffee did not. The findings, published in Nature Communications, suggest that caffeine may not be the main thing going on when coffee affects the brain. The Optimist Daily

Reported by The Optimist Daily

MoreLess

The experiment enrolled 31 regular coffee drinkers, who were asked to stop drinking their usual three to five cups a day for two weeks. The Optimist Daily Participants were then split: half received decaffeinated instant coffee, half received caffeinated, with neither group knowing which they had. The Optimist Daily

Both groups reported lower perceived stress, depression, and impulsivity after reintroducing coffee, regardless of caffeine content. The Optimist Daily Only the caffeinated group showed reductions in anxiety and improvements in attention and vigilance, consistent with decades of caffeine research. The Optimist Daily

How this works, and which outlets are included

These are inputs to compare, not sources to trust. Outlets are carried across the political spectrum, including state broadcasters, precisely so that a story can be read against itself.
