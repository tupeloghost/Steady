How this works, and which outlets are included

These are inputs to compare, not sources to trust. Outlets are carried across the political spectrum, including state broadcasters, precisely so that a story can be read against itself. Every outlet is labelled by lean, owner and money. Agreement across outlets that disagree is the only thing treated as evidence.

Each morning Steady reads news outlets from the left, center and right, plus government-funded and independent ones. Stories that several of them covered go first.

The quotes under each story are things outlets on different sides both reported, in their own words.
