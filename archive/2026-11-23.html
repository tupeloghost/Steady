Automatic abortion pardons clear women's records in England and Wales

BY THE OPTIMIST Daily EDITORIAL Team Women in England and Wales who were arrested, cautioned or convicted for ending their own pregnancies no longer carry those records. Automatic abortion pardons took effect on November 16, and police and courts must now delete the details of past convictions, cautions, arrests and investigations wherever they can. The Optimist Daily

Reported by The Optimist Daily

MoreLess

The women affected don't need to apply, pay a fee, or contact anyone. The Optimist Daily The pardons come under Section 242 of the Crime and Policing Act 2026, passed earlier this year alongside a separate provision that removed women from criminal law for ending their own pregnancies. The Optimist Daily Labour MP Tonia Antoniazzi, who brought forward the decriminalization amendment, says the change will clear the names of more than 100 women who were "unnecessarily harmed" by an "outdated" law. The Optimist Daily

The pardon applies to abortion offenses relating to a woman's own pregnancy, including cases where she acted beyond the legal time limits. The Optimist Daily Where full deletion from police and court systems isn't possible, records must be marked so they are treated as erased. The Optimist Daily Alex Davies-Jones, the minister for victims and tackling violence against women and girls, said the pardons had been a priority. The Optimist Daily

How this works, and which outlets are included

These are inputs to compare, not sources to trust. Outlets are carried across the political spectrum, including state broadcasters, precisely so that a story can be read against itself. Every outlet is labelled by lean, owner and money. Agreement across outlets that disagree is the only thing treated as evidence.

Each morning Steady reads news outlets from the left, center and right, plus government-funded and independent ones. Stories that several of them covered go first.

The quotes under each story are things outlets on different sides both reported, in their own words.

Headlines are reworded to remove charged language. Tap "See every outlet's headline" to read the originals.
